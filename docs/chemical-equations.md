# Chemical Equations — Master Checklist

A study checklist covering the Egyptian curriculum from secondary school (grades 10–12) through general university chemistry.

**How to use:** replace `☐` with `☑` in the tick column as you master each equation.

**Symbols:** `Δ` = heat · `↓` = precipitate · `↑` = gas evolved · `⇌` = reversible · `[O]` = oxidising agent · `[H]` = reducing agent · `(aq)` `(s)` `(l)` `(g)` = states · `conc.` = concentrated · `dil.` = dilute

## Study Notes

**Balancing:** conserve atoms of each element and total charge. For redox in acidic solution, balance O with H₂O, H with H⁺, then charge with e⁻. In basic solution, add OH⁻ to both sides to cancel H⁺.

**Common exam traps**

- Dilute HNO₃ with metals gives NO, not H₂ — nitric acid is an oxidiser.
- Fe + HCl gives Fe(II); Fe + Cl₂ gives Fe(III).
- SO₃ is dissolved in H₂SO₄ (oleum), never directly in water.
- Alcoholic KOH eliminates (alkene); aqueous KOH substitutes (alcohol).
- Cold dilute KMnO₄ gives a diol; hot concentrated KMnO₄ cleaves the double bond.
- 3° alcohols are not oxidised by acidified dichromate.
- Grignard reagents are destroyed by any source of acidic hydrogen (water, alcohols, acids).
